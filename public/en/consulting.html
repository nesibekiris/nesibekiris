What can we work on?

AI governance, technology policy, an idea you are developing. We define the question, examine the context you share and discuss practical next steps.

How does a session work?

A one-to-one session is 60 minutes, online. Send your topic and a short outline beforehand. We agree on fit, scope and fee, then schedule the conversation.

Where the session ends

This session is not legal representation or an organisational compliance audit. It does not include software development, tool implementation or certification. A broader corporate engagement needs a separate scope through STRATRI.
