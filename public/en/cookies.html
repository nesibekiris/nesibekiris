COOKIES

Cookies & browser storage

I have not added analytics, advertising pixels or marketing cookies to this site. The newsletter invitation uses localStorage, which is browser storage rather than a cookie.

Newsletter preference

The key techletter-invitation-dismissed stores only the time you close the invitation or click subscribe. It prevents automatic reminders for 30 days. It contains no name or email address and is not sent to me. You can reopen the invitation from the footer at any time.

Third-party services

ChatGPT Sites may use its own authentication or security mechanisms. Fonts connect to Google Fonts. Playing a video loads YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player; that service may use storage or process connection data. Following external links takes you to services with their own policies. This page describes the website code, not an inventory of all hosting-provider cookies.

Your browser controls

You can clear this site’s storage in your browser settings. The button below removes only the newsletter dismissal preference and reopens the invitation.
